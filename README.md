# print-designs

Print designs and services by CluoPrints — custom T-shirts, mugs, tote bags, posters and more.

CluoPrints offers custom apparel printing and gift printing for small businesses, teams, events and personal gifts. Send your logo, photo or idea, and CluoPrints will prepare a design and print it on your chosen product.

## Print services

- Custom apparel: T-shirts, sweatshirts and hoodies
- Gift printing: mugs, tote bags, posters, phone cases, pillows and notebooks
- Custom designs for companies, teams, events and families
- Original designs, made to order

## How to order

1. Browse the shop and choose a product, or send a custom design request.
2. For custom orders, email your logo or idea and your product requirements to info@cluoprints.com.
3. CluoPrints will confirm the design and printing details by email.

定制服装与礼品印刷服务：可通过邮件发送标志或创意并下单。
